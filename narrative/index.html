Featured Project

Within the Shadows

Format: Interactive Horror Narrative

Tool: Twine

Status: In Development

Within the Shadows is an interactive adaptation of an original horror story centered on the mysterious events surrounding Angel-Eye Woods. The project explores how atmosphere, player choice, pacing, and limited information can be used to create tension in an interactive format.

The initial playable sequence will adapt the story's opening encounter into a branching experience in which player decisions influence how the scene unfolds.

Design Focus:

  • Branching dialogue and player choice
  • Horror pacing and suspense
  • Environmental storytelling
  • Character perspective
  • Consequences and alternate outcomes
  • Interactive narrative structure

Playable build coming soon.

Narrative Design Goals

My narrative work focuses on translating traditional storytelling skills into interactive experiences. Rather than presenting the player with a single fixed sequence of events, these projects explore how choices, information, pacing, and consequence can affect the way a story is experienced.

Current areas of development include branching narrative structure, interactive dialogue, environmental storytelling, variable-based consequences, and designing choices that support character and atmosphere rather than functioning as simple menu options.
